# Kira Real Estate Product Requirements

## Product

Kira Real Estate is a production-quality demonstration website for presenting premium real estate web capabilities to Ethiopian developers, agencies, apartment sellers, and property managers. All listings and company information are sample content.

## Audience

- Property buyers browsing Addis Ababa homes and investment units
- Real estate decision-makers evaluating a future website partner
- Agency staff who need a clear, reusable lead-generation experience

## Core problem

Many property websites separate company credibility from useful property discovery. This demo combines a premium corporate presentation with working search, filtering, property detail, favorites, site-visit, WhatsApp, and contact flows.

## MVP scope

- Responsive homepage with search, featured listings, developments, services, locations, process, statistics, FAQ, and inquiry CTA
- Searchable and sortable `/properties` catalog with desktop and mobile filters
- Dynamic `/properties/[slug]` pages with gallery, lightbox, favorites, sharing, and inquiry actions
- Fully designed developments, about, and contact routes
- Local typed property data with at least 12 Addis Ababa listings
- Persistent favorites in localStorage
- Validated local forms with realistic success states
- SEO metadata, sitemap, robots, and structured data
- Accessible navigation, dialogs, drawers, and form controls
- EstateX-reference homepage reconstruction with original Kira branding and content
- Responsive service carousel, reveal motion, property collections, advisor grid, editorial news and CTA footer

## Success criteria

- All required routes resolve and link correctly
- Filtering, sorting, favorites, share, and forms behave correctly
- Desktop, tablet, and small-mobile layouts remain polished
- Lint, TypeScript, and production build pass
- Visual system feels bespoke, restrained, and sales-call ready
